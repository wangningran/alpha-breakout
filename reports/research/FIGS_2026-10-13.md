## Highlights
1. **Growth of 25%+ for 3 consecutive quarters, and it is broad-based.**
   - Q2 growth by segment:
     - scrubs +26.5%;
     - non-scrubs +40%;
     - US +22%;
     - international +67% (share up to 19%).
   - Two drivers: 3.1 million active customers (+13.2%), average order value $127 (+8.5%, mainly from price increases and product mix).
   - The company says revenue per active customer is $229, above the pandemic-era peak.
   - Re-acceleration from single-digit growth in 2024 to nearly 30% is the core of this surge.
2. **Clear operating leverage.**
   - Operating expense ratio down from 60.5% to 57.3%; stock-based compensation is declining (H1 $12.8 million vs. $15.0 million a year earlier).
   - Adjusted EBITDA margin 18.6%, vs. 12.9% a year earlier. This measure already excludes the $7.9 million of tariff refunds attributable to the prior year.
3. **Two "beat-and-raise" quarters.**
   - Full-year revenue guidance: +14~16% in May, raised to about +20% in August.
   - Full-year adjusted EBITDA margin guidance: 13.0~13.2% in May, raised to 14.8~15.0% in August.
4. **Clean balance sheet, buybacks started.**
   - Cash plus short-term investments of about $296 million, no financial debt.
   - H1 free cash flow $38.6 million (-$5.6 million a year earlier).
   - H1 buybacks of $32.8 million; in August the buyback authorization was increased by $100 million, about 3.4% of market cap.
   - Inventory $120 million, down YoY while revenue is up 29%, so inventory turnover is improving.
5. **Insiders.**
   - After the August surge, executives only had tax withholding on vesting (code F), with no open-market sales.
   - The 3 Form 4s on October 5 were all F, not sales.
   - Sales were concentrated in March–May: Executive Chair Hasson sold about $9.4 million after exercising options in March; the CEO and CFO made small sales in April–May, $160K–$900K per transaction.
   - Prices were around $14–15; the size was not large.

## Risks / where you need to make a judgment
1. **Gross margin only passes because of the tariff refund (important).**
   - The system's "gross margin YoY +8.2 pts" is propped up by a one-time refund; excluding it, it is only +0.4 pts, which does not satisfy v4's ≥ 2 pts rule.
   - Q1 was only +0.1 pts, and Q4'25 was still -4.4 pts (tariff hit).
   - So FIGS is a "revenue acceleration" type, not a "gross margin inflection" type.
   - This also exposes a loophole in the rules: XBRL data cannot separate out one-time items. Note it for now and consider handling it once the journal has accumulated more samples.
2. **The guidance implies a second-half slowdown.**
   - About +20% for the full year implies H2 revenue of about $400 million, only about +13% YoY (H1 was +28.5%).
   - This could be conservative guidance (they beat the last two times), or it could be a high base (Q4'25 grew 33%). Q3 earnings (expected early November) growth and guidance are key.
3. **Valuation is no longer cheap.** At 17.74:
   - Market cap: about $2.9 billion on about 166 million basic shares; about $3.5 billion on 195 million diluted shares.
   - EV about $2.6–3.2 billion.
   - Against 2026 guidance (revenue about $760 million, EBITDA about $113 million): EV/revenue about 3.5–4.2x, EV/EBITDA about 23–28x.
   - Trailing 12-month net income about $62 million, including the tariff refund; P/E about 47x (basic shares).
   - Diluted share count is 17% above the basic share count, so dilution from options and restricted stock is significant.
4. **Market cap is on the large side.** About $3 billion, near the upper bound of our small-cap definition, so less upside elasticity than smaller names.
5. **Reason for the 10-12 rally is unknown.** +12% that day on heavy volume of about 11.3 million shares (normally 2–4 million), with no new SEC filing. Suggest you check the news (analyst upgrades, tariff-related news, etc.).
6. **Earnings timing.** Q3 is expected in early November, about 3 weeks from now. After a probe, the position will very likely be held through earnings. The rules only avoid entering within 14 days before earnings; they do not avoid holding through earnings.
